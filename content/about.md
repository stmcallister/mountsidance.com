We are Mount Si — a 4A competitive high school dance & drill team based in the Snoqualmie
Valley.

We promote school spirit in the Snoqualmie Valley by performing at local events, football &
basketball games, and assemblies. Our competition team also competes at the local, state and
national level. Through dance, team members have the opportunity to be part of a proud
community that strives to learn and grow in their athleticism and artistry.
