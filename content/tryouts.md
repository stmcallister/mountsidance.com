## Tryouts to-do

1. **Fill out the [MSHS Athletic Registration](https://snoqualmievalley-wa.finalforms.com)**
   before tryouts. A current sports physical **must** be on file by the first day of tryouts in
   order to participate.
2. **Fill out the dance team application & agreement.**
3. **Attend tryouts in the MSHS main gym.** Please wear form-fitting athletic clothing, bring
   water, sneakers, and jazz shoes or half soles if you have them, and secure hair away from
   your face.
4. **Attend the parent meeting** during tryout week.

If you have any schedule conflicts, please contact the coach to see if we can work around it!
